DoseNag (working title)
A medication reminder that keeps reminding you until you check off the dose.
Status: validation — the app does not exist yet. This repository is the project's public home while I decide, with evidence, whether it is worth building. No code has been written. If the validation fails, this repo stays up as the record of the test and its results.
The problem
Most medication reminder apps alert you once, then go quiet. Swipe it away in a half-asleep moment and the dose is simply missed. At the same time, basic reminder features in popular apps keep moving behind subscriptions.
What I checked before proposing anything
I collected 54 firsthand user complaints and researched 23 existing products before forming this concept. Findings that shaped it:
Persistent re-alerting until a dose is confirmed exists today only in Android-only apps (e.g. Pillo, MedTimer).
Apple Health Medications follows up only once, about 30 minutes later.
Cross-platform apps (Medisafe, MyTherapy) offer snooze, not persistence, on their free tiers.
No product I checked documents "enter times-per-day and it works out the schedule" auto-spacing.
The concept (if validated)
Alerts repeat every few minutes until the dose is marked taken
One-tap "taken late" / skip, and edit a dose in place in seconds
Enter doses-per-day; the app works out the times
Data lives on the device; full export free forever, in standard formats
One-time price: $19.99. No subscription, ever
